WELCOME TO KLU University

KL University, officially known as Koneru Lakshmaiah Education Foundation (KLEF), is a deemed-to-be university located in Andhra Pradesh, India. It was established in 1980-81 as KL College of Engineering and later granted deemed university status in 2009. The university is known for its focus on academics, research, and innovation, with a strong emphasis on holistic development and global exposure. Here's a more detailed look at KL University: History and Recognition: Early Years: Started as KL College of Engineering in 1980-81. Autonomous Status: Became autonomous in 2006. Deemed University: Granted deemed-to-be university status in 2009 by the UGC, MHRD, Government of India. Category 1 University: Holds the prestigious Category I status as declared by the UGC. NIRF Ranking: Secured the 22nd position in the NIRF Rankings 2024. Key Features: Diverse Programs: Offers a wide range of undergraduate and postgraduate programs across various disciplines. Focus on Research and Innovation: Emphasizes research and innovation, fostering an environment of academic excellence. Strong Placement Record: Collaborates with top companies worldwide to provide excellent placement opportunities for students. Infrastructure: Boasts state-of-the-art infrastructure, including a large campus and modern facilities. Global Exposure: Provides opportunities for global exposure through twinning programs and partnerships with foreign universities. Entrepreneurship and Innovation: Supports budding entrepreneurs through a Technology Business Incubator and Innovation Cell. Location: Vijayawada: Located on the banks of the Krishna River, near Vijayawada, Andhra Pradesh. Green Fields Campus: The university campus is known as "Green Fields" and features a well-maintained environment with trees and gardens. Accessibility: The city is well-connected by road and rail. Recent Developments: Bribery Allegations: In February 2025, the university was at the center of a bribery investigation related to NAAC accreditation. CBI Raids: The CBI conducted raids at multiple locations in connection with the alleged bribery, including the recovery of cash and other valuables. Arrests: Several individuals associated with KL University were arrested in connection with the investigation, including the Vice-Chancellor and Vice-President.
